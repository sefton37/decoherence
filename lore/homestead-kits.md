# Homestead Kits

At the start of the game the player is given a choice how to specialize. They are picking a profession, and the choice does not give player skills, but gives them access to specific homestead kits/arrangements for that profession

1. Farming
   1. AgroDome (plant growth structure)
   2. Bioreactor (engineered microbes) - nitrogen, lipids
   3. Micronutrient biomass (vitamins, cofactors, etc)
   4. Consumable fiber biomass
   5. Textile fiber biomass
2. Mineral extraction.
   1. Regolith/bulk - scrapers, grinders - structural materials
   2. deep-core - drills, lances - concentrated ores
   3. fluid/brine - pumps, membranes - salts, battery metals
   4. atmospheric - skimmmers, cryo - gases, nitrogen
3. refining of farm resources - getting resources ready for production as inputs. Get various grades on purity based on effectiveness of refining process
   1. Cellular Protein Bioreactor - makes meat substitutes
4. refine mineral extraction
5. craft consumables
6. craft durables
7. potable water

## Sub-page: Modular Homestead Economy (not mirrored)

The Notion sub-page "Modular Homestead Economy" (https://app.notion.com/p/2d29fb31-ee83-8085-b460-cc24c1aca5ab)
is a long research write-up with two attached spreadsheets (`colony_goods_production.xlsx`,
`homestead_economy_matrix.xlsx`). It grounds each pod in real NASA/ESA/academic analogues
(RASSOR regolith excavation, TRIDENT drilling, brine electrodialysis, molten regolith electrolysis,
MOXIE, ISS ECLSS water recovery, on-orbit additive manufacturing). Its seven domains match the list
above: farming, mineral extraction, farm-resource refining, mineral-resource refining, crafting
consumables, crafting durables, potable water & life support. It is linked here rather than mirrored,
because it is reference research rather than lore canon. Mirror it if it becomes canon.
