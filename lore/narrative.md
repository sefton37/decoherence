# Narrative of Game

The player presses play.

Opens a loading screen for the Quantum Operating System by Your Empire

Prompts user to load a model for translation to your time and space

Connection made

Makes the case, we need people from your time and space to help manage colonization efforts of your empire.

Once you agree you are entangled with a colonist.

loads the terminal and cli - terminal is streaming scrollable output of your quantum universe

from the terminal the story plays out. Your colonist story is explained where they are going, the homestead chosen for them to settle. Then you get entangled, and all of your stats and vitals for the colonists come online

You colonist is in a landed drop pod, you need to provide the logic for assembling the components

learn the first CLI commands to switch to the other views

Now you switch to the view to program your colonist

Your colonist has a default survival loop,

- the colonist is in surival mode, so there is default anxiety loop that dominates
- gather enough food and water for the day
- on demand for water and food eat
